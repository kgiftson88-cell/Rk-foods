Everything is cooked fresh in small batches, from breakfast tiffin to full meals and evening tumblers of filter coffee.

Started by one family, one banana leaf at a time.

RK Foods began as a small tiffin counter run by Rajan and Kavitha, serving factory workers near Coimbatore before sunrise. Today the same recipes — ground on stone, cooked on wood-fed stoves at our central kitchen — reach your door through this menu.

  • Fresh daily — batter is ground each night, chutneys made each morning.
  • No shortcuts — sambar and rasam are simmered, not instant mixes.
  • Packed to travel — leak-proof boxes so idli stays soft and dosa stays crisp.
